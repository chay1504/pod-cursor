# Research, sources and attribution

Checked 5 November 2026. This project reconstructs an interaction from public sensor APIs/protocol descriptions. It is not the tweet creator's app. No claim is made that the tweet used exactly our sensor method.

- Requested demo: Andy Gao, https://x.com/instantricecook/status/2117802767557574717 . Tweet text recovered using FxTwitter because direct X access returned 403; video was not independently inspected. The author's personal site links GitHub account https://github.com/musubipapi ; its public repository listing did not expose a case-mouse implementation at the time inspected.
- Public Mac motion API: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmheadphonemotionmanager . Our `native/MotionBridge.swift` uses this API directly; no camera API.
- Independent case-control experiment: https://github.com/chroya/PodPointer . Reports open-lid use, buds inside, automatic ear detection disabled, rotational pointing and acceleration tap detection; also reports closed-lid disconnection. Used to understand feasibility and limitations. This new app does not bundle PodPointer or AirCursor.
- Another independent report: https://github.com/Winston774/air-mouse . Its author reports open-case testing on AirPods Pro 1/A2084/firmware 6F21. This is their test, not ours or a guarantee for every firmware.
- **MousePods by Matteo Gaudioso (starman-tech): https://github.com/starman-tech/mousepods .** Public protocol/layout findings and captures are licensed CC BY 4.0. Credit and license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . We implemented our own strict packet decoder and used the documented RemoteHID framing and 58-byte motion-block layout/scales. We did not copy its Linux application. File `tests/fixtures/recorded-motion.json` selects twelve unchanged motion-containing packets from `captures/airpods3_glide_raw2.json`, removing outer phase/time annotations; it includes source and license. The fixture is a sensor-decoding check, not evidence that AirPods 3 work inside a case. MousePods explicitly reports that its tested AirPods 3 powered their sensors off in-case.
- Windows interface/protocol reference: https://github.com/StarNumber12046/airpods-head-track/blob/main/src/bluetooth.rs and its README. Used to identify the MagicAAP interface GUID and required documented Win32 API sequence. Our ctypes transport is independently implemented. No driver is bundled.
- Additional Windows work: https://github.com/FIocker/aap-head-tracker . Its confirmed device is AirPods Max 2, not an earbud case; its other listed devices are experimental. We do not copy its GPL application or inherit its hardware validation claims.
- Driver maintainer: https://magicpods.app/magicaap/ and https://help.magicpods.app/fun-magicaap/ . Driver limitations explain the unresolved Windows hardware connection.
- Identify the exact AirPods: https://support.apple.com/en-us/109525 . On an iPhone look under Bluetooth > information button; otherwise look for the A-number printed on the earbud. Saved Bluetooth names alone are not reliable hardware identification.
- Android sensor availability: https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_motion . Gyro availability must be checked per model; no phone adapter is implemented here.
- DualSense gyro/accelerometer: https://blog.playstation.com/2020/11/09/ps5-the-ultimate-faq/comment-page-10/ . PC/Mac connection support: https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/pair-dualsense-controller-bluetooth/ . No controller adapter is implemented here.
- Joy-Con sensor specifications: https://www.nintendo.com/ph/hardware/switch/modal/specs/joy-con.html?width=960 . No Joy-Con adapter is implemented here.

The user-provided AirCursor repository and the prior camera prototype are not dependencies of this new project. Reference source downloads used for inspection remain in ignored local `research/` and are excluded from all distribution ZIPs.
